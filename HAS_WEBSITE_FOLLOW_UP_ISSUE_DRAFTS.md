# Has-Website Follow-Up 1 Drafts By Audit Issue

These are sample `follow_up_1` drafts for the has-website workflow. They use the approved direction:

- subject is `Re: [initial outreach subject]`
- the email mentions one specific issue from the audit
- the issue is explained in business/customer terms, not technical jargon
- the sign-off is compact
- the draft keeps `Original email platform` and `Outreach ID` for the manual sending workflow

---
